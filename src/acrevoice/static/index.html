Frequently asked questions

Does AcreVoice decide whether a farm is eligible?

No. It finds possible official guidance and prepares evidence. An authorised human adviser verifies any individual situation.

Is AcreVoice a government portal?

No. It is an independent support prototype that links to public government sources and prepares a hand-off for the official process.

What data is real?

Programme sources are public. The farms, fields, calls and answers in this hackathon demo are synthetic.

Can a German-speaking farmer use it?

Yes. The workspace supports German and English call language. The judge demo defaults to English for clarity.

AcreVoice is a hackathon prototype. It does not submit applications or make eligibility decisions.
